Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need to pass KYC or submit social tasks to participate?
No. The HOLDMYBEER airdrop is 100% on-chain. You do not need to provide personal identification, connect Twitter or Discord, or complete repetitive chores. Holding HMB on a non-custodial Polygon address is the only requirement.
Why does 200,000,000 HMB not guarantee an airdrop payout?
Because 200M HMB is strictly the qualification threshold (Filter #1). Only the Top-100 highest balances among qualified participants at the snapshot will receive rewards (Filter #2).
Is there a risk of Sybil bans if I use multiple wallets?
No. The protocol does not ban users for managing multiple wallets. However, splitting tokens across several addresses dilutes your balance per wallet, significantly reducing your chances of ranking in the Top-100.
When will the remaining 19 airdrops take place?
Each subsequent round is announced sequentially following the complete settlement of the preceding event. Terms and allocation schedules will be published on this portal and on official channels.
